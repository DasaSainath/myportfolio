// About Me

A bit about me

Generative AI • LLMs • RAG • Deep Learning • Cloud ML — and a lot of curiosity

I'm a Data Scientist with 5+ years of experience across Machine Learning, Generative AI, Deep Learning, and Data Science. I didn't get here by accident — I started in statistics, fell in love with what data could explain, and then couldn't stop once machine learning entered the picture.

I work mostly with LLMs, LangChain, Python, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Snowflake, and AWS. The work I'm proudest of so far hit 92% retrieval accuracy, cut screening time by 70%, and reduced cost by 40% — not because the numbers look good, but because real people got hours of tedious work back.

What drives me is simple: I like building things end to end, from a messy dataset to a system running in production that someone actually uses — data preparation and feature development, model building and evaluation, vector search, cloud deployment, and monitoring, with clean, scalable engineering using Docker, Lambda, and Terraform. If it lives only in a notebook, I haven't finished the job.

// My Journey

From Hyderabad to Delaware

Every step taught me something different — about data, and about myself.

Hyderabad is home, and it's where my story starts. I studied statistics there — first my B.Sc. in Mathematics, Statistics & Computer Science, then my M.Sc. in Applied Statistics & Computer Science. Numbers were never abstract to me; they were how I learned to think.

I started my career in Hyderabad too — first at Amazon, working on Document AI, and then at Capgemini, working on ADAS data for automotive AI. That's where I learned how much model quality depends on the unglamorous work: clean data, careful annotation, and honest validation.

In 2023, I moved to the US to do my M.S. in Information Systems & Technologies at Wilmington University, which I finished in 2025. Moving countries on your own changes you — it made me more independent, more resourceful, and hungrier to build.

Now I live in Newark, Delaware, work as a Data Scientist, and in my own time I'm the founder of ShapeFuel AI — because building things is what I do, whether someone assigns it to me or not.

// How I Work

What I believe

A few principles I keep coming back to, on every project.

Ship it, then improve it. A working system in real hands teaches me more than a perfect plan ever will.

Data quality before model cleverness. My annotation and QA years taught me that no architecture rescues bad data.

Measure the impact, not just the accuracy. A model matters when it saves someone time, money, or frustration — that's the metric I chase.

Stay curious, stay humble. This field moves fast. I'd rather ask a good question than pretend I already know.
