a little about me

My story, in my own words

Numbers were my first love. I grew up in Hyderabad, India, and went all-in on them — a B.Sc. and then an M.Sc. in Statistics & Computer Science. I didn't just want to study data, though. I wanted to see what it could do.

So I learned in the real world: at Amazon, teaching machines to read documents, and at Capgemini, working on the data behind self-driving cars. That's where I got obsessed with the gap between a model that works in a notebook and one that survives real life.

In 2023 I moved to the US for my M.S. at Wilmington University (finished 2025). Now I'm in Newark, Delaware, building GenAI at Gig Health Care — and still building my own things on the side, because I honestly can't help it. — that's the whole pattern, really: learn it, then build it.
